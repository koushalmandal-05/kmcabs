Refund Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This Refund Policy applies to paid bookings facilitated by KM Enterprises under the KM Cabs brand.

1. Advance payments

If an advance or full payment was collected and the trip is cancelled as per our Cancellation Policy, eligible amounts will be refunded to the original payment method where possible.

2. Non-refundable items

  • Completed trips
  • Tolls, parking, permits or third-party charges already incurred
  • No-show situations after the agreed waiting window

3. Service issues

If a confirmed vehicle is not provided due to reasons attributable to KM Cabs or our assigned partner, we will offer a suitable alternative or a refund of the unused prepaid amount.

4. Processing time

Approved refunds are typically initiated within 7–10 business days. Bank or wallet timelines may vary.

5. How to request a refund

Email koushalmandal05@gmail.com with your name, phone number, booking reference (if any), trip date and reason for the request.
