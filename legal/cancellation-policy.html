Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Cancellations for KM Cabs services in Bokaro, Ranchi and Kolkata are handled as follows.

1. Before confirmation

Callback requests that have not been confirmed as bookings can be withdrawn anytime by informing us at koushalmandal05@gmail.com or during the callback itself.

2. After confirmation

  • City cab / goods: Free cancellation up to 15 minutes before the scheduled pickup, unless a vehicle has already been dispatched and waiting charges apply.
  • Rental: Free cancellation up to 2 hours before the package start time; later cancellations may attract a partial package charge.
  • Outstation: Free cancellation up to 6 hours before departure; later cancellations may attract a fee communicated at booking time.

3. Driver cancellations

If we or our partner cancel a confirmed trip, we will attempt to arrange an alternative vehicle or cancel without penalty to you.

4. No-show

If the passenger or consignor is unreachable after a reasonable wait at the pickup point, the trip may be marked as a no-show and cancellation/waiting charges may apply.

5. Contact

To cancel, email koushalmandal05@gmail.com with your name, phone and trip details.
