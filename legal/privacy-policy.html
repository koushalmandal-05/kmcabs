Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how KM Enterprises (“KM Cabs”, “we”, “us”) collects and uses information when you use our website and request services in Bokaro, Ranchi and Kolkata.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact details you submit (name, phone number, email)
  • Trip details (city, pickup, destination, service type, selected vehicle, estimated fare)
  • Optional notes you provide in callback or contact forms
  • Basic technical data such as browser type and pages visited (via hosting/analytics tools if enabled)

2. How we use information

  • To respond to fare and callback requests
  • To confirm bookings and provide customer support
  • To improve website performance and service quality

3. Sharing of information

We may share trip and contact details with drivers or partner operators solely to fulfil your request. We may also use trusted processors (for example form/email delivery and hosting providers) to operate the website. We do not sell personal information.

4. Data retention

We retain enquiry and booking-related information for as long as needed to provide service, resolve disputes, and meet legal or accounting requirements.

5. Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

7. Children’s privacy
