About KM Cabs

A KM Enterprises mobility brand built for clear fares and local service.

Who we are

KM Cabs helps people and businesses move across Bokaro, Ranchi and Kolkata with city cabs, rentals, outstation trips and goods transport. We keep the booking flow simple: enter your route, compare vehicle fares, and request a callback.

Our approach

We show estimated fares up front, including a small platform fee between ₹0.75 and ₹0.99. A team member confirms availability, timing and final charges when they call you back.

Where we operate

Services are available only in Bokaro, Ranchi and Kolkata. We do not currently accept bookings from other cities.
